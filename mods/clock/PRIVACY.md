# Clock privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Clock is a Claude Code mod by hacksurvivor. It runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, no account and no analytics, and its author receives nothing from it.

## What it reads

Your computer's date, time and timezone; your prompts, to see which period you ask about; the session's folder; and `~/.claude/clock/markets.json` when it exists.

## What it sends, and where

Clock sends nothing itself. It adds a short brief (your local date and time, the week ranges and your markets' times) to each prompt, which Claude Code sends to **Anthropic under your own Claude account**. See https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

## What it keeps

Nothing after the session ends.

## Children

Clock is not intended for people under 18.

Open an issue at https://github.com/hacksurvivor/claude-mods/issues.
