# Third eye privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Third eye is a Claude Code mod by hacksurvivor. It runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, no account and no analytics, and its author receives nothing from it.

## What it reads

Your current Claude Code conversation, when it looks for an idea: through a fork of the conversation that Claude Code sends to your session's model, and the last reply's text when you ask after a reload.

## What it sends, and where

Only that fork, to **Anthropic, through Claude Code under your own Claude account**, the same way any turn of your conversation is sent. Anthropic handles it under your account's terms and settings: see https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Third eye sends nothing anywhere else.

## What it keeps

In Claude Code's plugin store on your computer, until you remove the mod: the last 20 ideas it showed, and up to 30 ideas you turned down and 30 you took up, to steer the next ones.

## Children

Third eye is not intended for people under 18.
