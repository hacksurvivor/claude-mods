# Bouncer privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Bouncer is a Claude Code mod by hacksurvivor. It runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, no account and no analytics, and its author receives nothing from it.

## What it reads

Your prompts, to recognise a pasted link with a short question, and your project list in `~/.claude/bouncer/projects.md` when it exists.

## What it sends, and where

Bouncer sends nothing itself. When you paste a link it adds a brief to your prompt (the link, your project list and the format of the verdict), which Claude Code sends to **Anthropic under your own Claude account** with your message. See https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Claude may open the link with its own tools, as it would without Bouncer.

## What it keeps

On your computer, in Claude Code's plugin store, until you remove the mod: up to 200 links it judged, with their verdict and your choice.

## Children

Bouncer is not intended for people under 18.
